Report: Hezbollah Has Not Given Guarantees It Won't Intervene in War

BEIRUT —

A senior Hezbollah official told al-Liwaa newspaper that reports claiming the group pledged to stay out of a possible Iran-Israel-US confrontation are false, saying any decision to join a war depends on battlefield developments and Israeli actions. The report said Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Saeed Khatibzadeh did not raise the issue during his recent visit to Beirut.

Microsoft Offers Buyouts to Around 60,000 Employees

Microsoft confirmed it is offering voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees as part of its ongoing cost-cutting push.
